Daily closes (UTC) from Coinbase Exchange, stored in data/prices.json (seeded Oct 31, 2026 00:45 UTC). The latest day may be partial. Dashed line: entry price.

Methodology

Entry = mean of the closing prices of each one-minute BTC-USD candle in the window. BTC amount = $10,000 ÷ entry, rounded to 8 decimals. Current value = BTC amount × current spot price. Live price: Coinbase spot (api.coinbase.com/v2/prices/BTC-USD/spot), falling back to CoinGecko (api.coingecko.com/api/v3/simple/price), then to the most recent daily close in data/prices.json. Data files: position.json, prices.json.

Tracking window: October 30 – November 15, 2026.
